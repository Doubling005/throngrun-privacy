Throng Run - Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 November 2026 · App: Throng Run (com.doubling005.throngrun)

This policy explains what information the Throng Run game ("the Game") uses, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. "We" and "us" mean the developer of Throng Run, as named on the Game's Google Play listing. You can open this policy at any time from Settings > Privacy policy in the Game. Please also read our Terms of Use.

In short: we don't collect your personal information. Your progress is saved on your phone and, if you use Google Play Games, backed up to your own Play Games account, which we cannot see. Google AdMob shows ads and may use your device's advertising ID (with your consent where the law requires it). Google Play handles all payments.

1. Information we collect

We do not run servers for the Game and we do not collect, receive, store or sell your personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no chat and no way for players to contact each other.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following on your device so it can work (and, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, backs it up as described in section 3). It is never sent to us:

  • your progress: levels, stars, coins, gems, upgrades, characters, skins and statistics;
  • your settings: sound and music volume, vibration and graphics quality;
  • a record of your Google Play purchases (the purchase token and order number Google Play gives the Game), so that items are granted once and one-time purchases can be restored;
  • simple ad counters (for example how many free-gem ads you watched today), so ad limits work;
  • which version of these policies you accepted, and when.

You can delete it from your device at any time with Settings > Reset progress, by clearing the Game's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the Game.

3. Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the Game keeps a copy of the progress listed in section 2 in the "Saved Games" area of your Google Play Games account, so you can get it back after reinstalling the Game or on a new phone. This copy is stored and protected by Google in your account; we cannot access it. To provide this, Google processes your Play Games profile (such as your player ID and gamer name) under the Google Privacy Policy; the Game does not read or keep your profile.

The cloud copy is optional. If you are not signed in, nothing is backed up and you can sign in at any time from Settings > Cloud save. Settings > Reset progress also replaces the cloud copy (within about a minute). To delete Play Games data entirely, use the Google Play Games settings on your device or your Google account (myaccount.google.com).

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver, measure and limit ads and to prevent ad fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • the device's advertising ID (AD_ID) and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads (views, clicks), and diagnostics.

Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent with Google's consent tool before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options (shown where it applies). Without consent, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use limited data.

Your controls: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads or Google > Ads, depending on your device). Buying Remove Ads stops the automatic ads between levels; optional rewarded ads stay available if you choose to watch them.

How Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We never see your name, card, UPI or other payment details. The Game receives only a confirmation of the purchase (product, purchase token, order number and Google's signature) so it can deliver what you bought.

6. Google Play statistics

Like every app on Google Play, Google provides us with aggregated statistics, such as numbers of installs, ratings and anonymous crash and performance reports collected by Android and Google Play. These do not identify you.

8. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Players under 18 should use the Game with a parent's or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly allow personal information of children under 13 to be collected. If you believe a child under 13 is using the Game, or that information about a child has been collected through ads, contact us and we will help, including asking Google to delete it.

9. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information, because we don't collect any. Information collected by Google for ads and purchases is handled under Google's own policies linked above.

10. Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you reset progress, clear the Game's data or uninstall the Game. The cloud copy stays in your Play Games account until you replace it (by playing or resetting) or delete your Play Games data. Data processed by Google is kept and deleted under Google's policies; you can manage it at myaccount.google.com and reset your advertising ID as described above.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data about you, most requests concern data held by Google, which you can manage with your Google account and the controls above. You can always contact us (section 15) and we will help you exercise your rights.

12. Security

Game data stays inside the Game's private storage on your device and, if you use cloud save, in your own Google Play Games account. Communication with Google's services is encrypted (HTTPS) by Google's software. No method is perfectly secure, but we never hold your data on our side.

13. International processing

Google may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, under the safeguards described in Google's privacy policy.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and important changes will be shown in the Game.

15. Contact and grievances

Questions, requests or complaints about privacy: doublingentertainment@gmail.com (please put "Throng Run privacy" in the subject). We aim to acknowledge within 2 working days and to respond within 30 days.
