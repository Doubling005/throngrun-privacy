Throng Run - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · App: Throng Run (com.doubling005.throngrun) · Developer: Doubling005

This policy explains what information Throng Run ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information the developer collects

The developer does not run any servers for the game and does not collect, receive or store your personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no chat.

Your progress (coins, gems, levels, upgrades, settings and purchases you have made) is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the game asks for your consent first using Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

How Google uses this information is described in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and the Google Privacy Policy https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details. The game receives only a purchase confirmation from Google Play so it can deliver what you bought.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game in a way that shared personal information through ads, contact us and we will help.

5. Data sharing and selling

The developer does not sell or share your personal information. Information collected by Google for ads and purchases is handled under Google's own policies linked above.

6. Security and retention

Game data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game. Data processed by Google is retained under Google's policies.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

8. Contact
