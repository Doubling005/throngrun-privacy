Throng Run - Delete your data

App: Throng Run: Crowd Battle 3D (com.doubling005.throngrun) · Developer: as named on the game's Google Play listing

In short: we (the developer) do not hold any of your data on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete. Your game data is on your phone and, if you use cloud save, in your own Google Play Games account. You can delete both yourself with the steps below, at any time. If you need help, email us and we will guide you.

1. Delete your game data on your phone

Use any one of these:

  1. In the game, open Settings > Reset progress and confirm. This deletes coins, gems, upgrades, unlocks and statistics on the phone (purchases such as Remove Ads stay restorable through Google Play).
  2. Or open Android Settings > Apps > Throng Run > Storage > Clear data.
  3. Or uninstall the game.

This deletes it immediately. Nothing is kept by us.

2. Delete your cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you used cloud save, a copy of your progress is stored in your Google Play Games account. To delete it:

  1. Open your Play Games profile (or the Play Games section of your Google account settings on your phone).
  2. Find Throng Run in your games list and choose to delete its data, or delete your Play Games profile entirely.

Using Settings > Reset progress in the game while signed in also replaces the cloud copy with an empty save within about a minute. Google deletes the data under its own retention rules; see the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Data used by Google for ads

Ads in the game are provided by Google AdMob. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads), and manage or delete data Google holds about you at myaccount.google.com.

4. What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your progress, coins, gems, upgrades, characters, skins, statistics, settings and ad counters (on the phone, and in the cloud copy if you delete it as above).
  • Kept by Google Play, not by us: records of your Google Play purchases, which Google keeps under its own policies so that one-time purchases (such as Remove Ads) can be restored.
  • We keep no copy of any of your data, so no retention period applies on our side.

5. Ask us for help

Email doublingentertainment@gmail.com with "Throng Run delete my data" in the subject. We will reply within 30 days with help for your device. Please don't send passwords or payment details.

See also our Privacy Policy.
